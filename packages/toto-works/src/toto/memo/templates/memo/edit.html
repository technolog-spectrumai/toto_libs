{% comment %}---- the box dialog ---- One modal, per content type, opened from the box's Edit button. Everything a kind of content needs to be written — LaTeX, an SVG, a listing, a picture — is a small form, and four small forms scattered across a 1280x720 slide is what made the canvas unusable at the size it is actually shown.
