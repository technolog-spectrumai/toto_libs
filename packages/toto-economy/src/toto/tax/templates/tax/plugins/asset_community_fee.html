A holding fee is keyed by ASSET — a share of what you hold above a threshold — so unlike a levy allowance it cannot live on a metered thing. It lives here, where everything else true of this asset lives, and staff edit it in place. It used to be the bottom half of a page called "Allowances" whose top half configured levy allowances: two unrelated objects, one save button. A member sees the section only when the fee is ACTIVE. Staff always see it, so one can be created — and so a paused fee does not vanish from the only page that could un-pause it.{% endcomment %}
