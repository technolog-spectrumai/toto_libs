{% comment %} THE PIE: the files area against its budget — the one disk figure with both a measured use and a limit the executor enforces. The budget is the executor's own constant, passed in, never restated here. Job input and output is NOT a slice: it lives outside that budget and has no total of its own, so it is a line under the pie. The reserved Scratch is not drawn at all — it is memory, per runner, and nothing measures how much of it is used. OVER BUDGET the ring is full and amber and a sentence says by how much: a pie cannot show more than all of itself, and a full ring alone would read as "exactly full". INCOMPLETE counts as over budget, because the executor counts it so (`executor/files.py:check_area_budget`): what was counted is drawn, the rest of the ring is amber and "not counted", and the sentence says nothing more is accepted. Drawing the rest as free space would promise room the executor refuses. {% endcomment %}
